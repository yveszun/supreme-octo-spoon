# ReferAll Demo Script - Hackachusetts 2026
**Duration: 5-7 minutes | Healthcare Track**

---

## Opening (30 seconds)
**"Hi, I'm [name] and I built ReferAll - a healthcare referral coordination platform that solves a critical but invisible problem in our healthcare system."**

**The Problem:** When your doctor refers you to a specialist, that referral can get stuck for weeks or months. Maybe the specialist doesn't take your insurance, or you can't get there without a car, or there's a language barrier. Care coordinators manage hundreds of these referrals manually, with no visibility into system-wide bottlenecks.

---

## Demo Flow (4-5 minutes)

### 1. Dashboard Overview (45 seconds)
**"Let me show you ReferAll in action. This is a coordinator's view with real Massachusetts hospitals and synthetic patient data."**

**Point to top stats bar:**
- "87 hospitals from CMS data"
- "25 active referrals being tracked"
- "Two critical metrics: Actions Completed (what coordinators do) and Barriers Resolved (patient outcomes)"

**Point to three-column layout:**
- "Hospital dashboard on left"
- "Referral tracker front and center - because that's the coordinator's workflow"
- "Barrier analysis on right"

### 2. High-Priority Referral (90 seconds)
**"Notice these priority badges. Let me show you one marked 'High Admin Priority'..."**

**Hover over priority badge:**
- "This tooltip shows exactly why it's prioritized - 42 days waiting, 3 barriers, multiple contact attempts"
- "This is rule-based scoring, not AI - completely explainable"

**Click "View Details" on Sarah Chen's referral:**
- "Sarah needs cardiology but has three barriers: transportation, insurance verification, and language"
- "ReferAll generates a coordination plan with specific next steps"

**Click through coordination plan actions:**
- "Check one: Arrange medical transport through MassHealth"
- "Check two: Contact interpreter services"
- "These completions feed into our Actions Completed metric"

**Scroll to show barriers:**
- "Once all actions for a barrier are done AND the coordinator confirms resolution, it counts toward Barriers Resolved"
- "This distinction matters - we track both coordinator work and patient outcomes"

### 3. System-Level Intelligence (90 seconds)
**"Now here's what makes ReferAll different from a simple task tracker..."**

**Click to expand "Referral Bottleneck Intelligence":**
- "This connects individual patient needs with health system problems"
- "Transportation is our Critical bottleneck - affecting 12 patients, average 31 days stuck"
- "But look at the system-level solution: standardized transport eligibility with payer partnerships"

**Point to Health Systems Recommendations box:**
- "ReferAll identifies who needs to be at the table - payers, hospitals, transport providers"
- "And what the expected impact is - 40% reduction in referral delays"

**Point to Key Insights:**
- "Insurance barriers affect high-acuity specialties most"
- "Communication barriers cluster in multilingual communities"
- "These insights help administrators allocate resources strategically"

### 4. Workflow Design (30 seconds)
**"One design choice I'm proud of: this intelligence section is collapsible and below the operational dashboard."**

**Collapse the section:**
- "Coordinators need referrals visible immediately - that's their daily workflow"
- "Strategic analysis is one click away when managers need it"
- "Workflow-first design for real users"

---

## Closing (30 seconds)
**"ReferAll gives coordinators actionable next steps for individual patients, while giving administrators visibility into system-wide bottlenecks. It's not just tracking referrals - it's identifying where our healthcare system breaks down and how to fix it."**

**"Built with real Massachusetts hospital data, clinically appropriate terminology, and honest technical claims - explainable intelligence, actionable coordination, privacy-conscious design."**

**"Questions?"**

---

## Backup Slides (if asked to show more)

### Show Hospital Dashboard:
- Filter by specialty or distance
- Real MA hospital data with specialties

### Show Barrier Analysis Chart:
- Pie chart of barrier distribution
- Click through to affected referrals

### Show Modal Scrollability:
- Open referral with long coordination plan
- Demonstrate smooth scrolling with fixed header

---

## Time Management
- **2 min warning:** Wrap up bottleneck intelligence, go to closing
- **1 min warning:** Skip to closing immediately
- **Over time:** Cut hospital dashboard demo, focus on priority scoring + bottleneck intelligence

---

## Energy & Pacing
- **Enthusiastic but clear** - you're solving a real problem
- **Pause after key points** - let judges absorb the dual perspective (patient + system)
- **Point with cursor** - keep judges' eyes following your demo flow
- **Smile when showing insights** - you're proud of the intelligence layer

---

## Recovery Lines
**If browser freezes:** "This is a single-file HTML app with no server - let me refresh... there we go."

**If judges look confused:** "Think of it this way: every referral is a patient waiting. ReferAll helps coordinators unblock individual patients while showing administrators where the system needs fixing."

**If asked about data:** "87 real Massachusetts hospitals from CMS Hospital Compare. 25 synthetic patient referrals with diverse barriers based on healthcare coordination research."

---

## What NOT to Say
❌ "This would be HIPAA compliant" (it's not production-ready)
❌ "AI-powered" (it's rule-based)
❌ "This could integrate with Epic/Cerner" (true but unbuilt)
✅ "This demonstrates explainable priority scoring"
✅ "Built with clinical appropriateness in mind"
✅ "Designed for real coordinator workflows"
