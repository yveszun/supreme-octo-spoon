💬 Q&A - YOUR BEST ANSWERS

Q: "What makes this different from ReferralMD?"
A: "Three things: First, transparent priority triage—hover over
    any badge to see exact reasoning. Second, personalized
    coordination plans with progress tracking. Third, this
    bottleneck intelligence section—ReferralMD tracks cases,
    we provide health systems strategy. We bridge operational
    and strategic."

Q: "Is this using AI?"
A: "No—explainable intelligence with rule-based algorithms.
    See the header? We're honest about it. Priority is calculated
    by explicit scoring. Hover over a badge—see the formula
    applied. No ML, no neural networks. In healthcare,
    explainability and auditability matter more than
    sophistication."

Q: "Why did you reorganize the layout?"
A: "Healthcare coordinators need immediate access to their
    patients. Before, strategic intelligence pushed referrals
    below the fold. Now: referrals visible immediately,
    strategic analysis one click away. The layout matches
    the actual workflow. That's good design: form follows
    function."

Q: "How long did this take to build?"
A: "Under 3 hours. We focused on understanding the problem
    first—talked to healthcare coordinators, studied existing
    tools, identified gaps. Then built with clear requirements.
    Speed came from understanding the domain, not cutting
    corners."

Q: "Is this HIPAA compliant?"
A: "We use synthetic data and privacy-conscious design. For
    production, you'd need proper HIPAA infrastructure—
    encryption, audit logs, BAAs, security assessment. We
    don't claim compliance without proper evaluation. That's
    why the header says 'Privacy-Conscious Design' not
    'HIPAA-Ready.' Honest claims matter."

Q: "Can you show me the code?"
A: "Absolutely. Single HTML file—self-contained, easy to
    review. Priority calculation function is fully visible.
    Or just hover over any badge—the tooltip shows the exact
    logic applied to that patient. Or open console, type
    calculatePriority(referrals[0]). Full transparency."

Q: "Who would use this?"
A: "Two audiences: Care coordinators use the referral tracker
    daily—manage patients, generate plans, track progress.
    Leadership uses the bottleneck intelligence—identify
    systemic barriers, plan policy interventions, allocate
    resources. One platform serves both operational and
    strategic needs."

Q: "What's next for ReferAll?"
A: "Three priorities: First, pilot with a health system to
    validate workflow. Second, backend API for real EHR
    integration (HL7 FHIR). Third, ML for barrier prediction—
    but only after we have training data and proper evaluation.
    We'd add ML thoughtfully, not just for hype."
